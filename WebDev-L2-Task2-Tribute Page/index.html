01

HER STORY

Education became her revolution.

Savitribai Phule was born on 3 January 1831 in Naigaon, Maharashtra. She grew up during a period when education was largely denied to women and many marginalized communities. Her journey would eventually challenge those social barriers.

With the support and encouragement of Jyotirao Phule, she learned to read and write and became a teacher. Together, they worked to make education accessible to girls at a time when the idea itself faced strong opposition.

In 1848, Savitribai and Jyotirao Phule started a school for girls at Bhide Wada in Pune. Savitribai became one of the earliest modern women teachers in India. Her classroom became a symbol of courage, learning and social change.

Her contribution went beyond education. She worked for women, widows and oppressed communities and responded to suffering during the plague epidemic of 1896–97. Her life continues to represent the power of education, equality and compassion.
